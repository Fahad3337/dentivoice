FAQ

Questions Dental Clinic Owners Ask Us

How an AI receptionist talks to your patients, works with your team, and fits into your practice.

Will patients know they're talking to an AI receptionist?

add

Dentivoice introduces itself as the clinic's virtual assistant. The voice is warm and natural, there's no phone-tree menu, and callers get an instant answer instead of voicemail. Most patients care far more about getting booked than who picks up.

Does it replace my front desk team?

add

No. It handles overflow, lunch-hour, and after-hours calls, plus repetitive questions, so your team can focus on the patients in the chair and at the counter.

What happens if the AI can't answer a question?

add

It transfers the call to your staff, or takes a detailed message and sends it to your team right away. You decide the rules for when a human takes over.

Does it work with Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental?

add

Dentivoice books into your calendar from day one. Direct integration with Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and other practice management software is available. We'll confirm what works for your setup on the demo call.

How does it handle dental emergencies?

add

It asks screening questions about pain, swelling, and bleeding, books urgent slots when available, and alerts your on-call dentist for serious cases. Anyone describing a medical emergency is told to call 911.

How is patient information protected?

add

Dentivoice is built for HIPAA workflows. We'll walk you through exactly how call data is stored, secured, and accessed on your demo call.

How much does it cost?

add

A flat monthly fee based on your call volume. Every clinic starts with a free missed-call audit, with no contract.

How long does setup take?

add

About 2 to 3 weeks. We learn your hours, services, providers, and insurance panels, build your custom voice agent, and you simply forward your calls.
